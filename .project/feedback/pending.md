## How to Use This File

When human feedback is received, add it here:

```markdown
### [FB-XXX] Brief Title
**Received**: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM
**Source**: Human name or session
**Priority**: High | Medium | Low

> Quoted feedback from human

**Status**: Pending | In Progress | Incorporated | Declined
**Assigned To**: arm-name or Unassigned
**Notes**: Any additional context

**Action Taken**: (fill in when addressed)
**Completed**: (fill in date when done)
```

When feedback is addressed, move to `incorporated.md` with completion details.
